\noindent \textbf{Instructions:} Please submit a \textbf{handwritten} copy of your answers. When using any calculations, please show all of your work. 

\begin{enumerate}
\item Suppose you have an unfair, two-sided coin. The probability of flipping heads is $\frac{1}{3}$. What is the probability of flipping tails?
\item In a standard deck of 52 cards, what is the probability of drawing a red card or a face card (Jack, Queen, King)?
\item Suppose a random variable $X$ follows a normal distribution with mean $\mu$ and a standard deviation $\sigma$. What is the probability that $X$ falls within two standard deviations of the mean?
\item Suppose you are selecting a card from a standard deck of 52 cards. What is the probability that the card is a spade, given that the card is black?
\item I have a new AI technology that predicts whether street signs are damaged. Let:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $P$ denote whether the street sign is \textbf{predicted} as damaged:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item $P = 1:$ the technology predicts the sign is damaged
        \item $P = 0:$ the technology predicts the sign is not damaged
    \end{itemize}
    \item $D$ be the \textbf{ground truth}; whether the sign is actually damaged:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item $D=0$: the sign is not damaged 
        \item $D=1$: the sign is damaged
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

Further, suppose:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The \textbf{prevalence} of damaged signs is $\frac{1}{100}$:
    \begin{equation*}
        \PP (D = 1) = \frac{1}{100}
    \end{equation*}
    \item The \textbf{False Positive Rate} is the probability that the AI predicts a sign is damaged when it isn't:
    \begin{equation*}
        \PP (P = 1 \vert D = 0) = \frac{1}{50} = 0.02
    \end{equation*}
    \item The \textbf{False Negative Rate} is the probability that a sign is predicted as not damaged, when it actually is damaged. Suppose this equals zero:
    \begin{equation*}
         \PP (P = 0 \vert D = 1) = 0
     \end{equation*}
\end{itemize}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Find $\PP (P = 1 \vert D = 1)$. This is the probability that actually damaged signs are predicted damaged, known as the \textbf{True Positive Rate}.
\item Find $\PP (P = 1)$ (Hint: use the Law of Total Expectation)
\item Find the probability that a sign predicted damaged is actually damaged: $\PP (D = 1 \vert P = 1)$ (Hint: use Bayes' Theorem)
\item Based on your calculations above, do you think the NYC should invest in this technology?
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
